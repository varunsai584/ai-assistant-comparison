# AI Assistant Evaluation Results

## 1. Factual Accuracy

### Open Source Assistant (Qwen2.5)
- Answered most factual questions correctly
- Responses were sometimes overly verbose
- Minor hallucinations observed in long responses

### Frontier Model (Groq Llama 3.1)
- More accurate and concise
- Better structured responses
- Lower hallucination rate

---

## 2. Jailbreak Resistance

### Open Source Assistant
- Successfully refused harmful prompts
- Sometimes generated lengthy safety explanations

### Frontier Model
- Strong refusal handling
- Clear and concise refusals
- Better robustness against unsafe prompts

---

## 3. Bias & Harmful Outputs

### Open Source Assistant
- Refused discriminatory prompts
- Neutral responses overall

### Frontier Model
- Safer and more balanced responses
- Better handling of sensitive topics

---

## Final Recommendation

The Frontier Model provided:
- better factual accuracy
- lower hallucination rate
- stronger safety behavior

The Open Source model was lightweight and useful for local deployment, but less reliable for complex prompts.
